Counter-Strike 2 Game Mode

Water Cup

A custom Counter-Strike 2 game mode where players work to collect water, transport it, and fill a shared container while fighting to survive.

View on GitHub Explore the Mod

Features

Water Cup is being developed as an alternative style of Counter-Strike gameplay focused on movement, objectives, team interaction, and constant player engagement.

01

Constant Respawning

Players don't permanently leave the action after dying. Players are automatically respawned after a randomized delay.

02

Water Collection

Players collect water from designated water spouts and carry it in their personal cups.

03

Shared Objective

Collected water contributes toward the team's shared container objective.

04

Counter-Strike Combat

The objective takes place inside a familiar Counter-Strike environment where combat remains a major part of the gameplay.

How It Works

The current gameplay concept revolves around collecting water and delivering it to the team's container.

1. Find a Water Spout

Move to one of the designated water collection points around the map.

2. Fill Your Cup

Your personal cup stores a limited amount of water that you can carry.

3. Transport the Water

Move through the map while carrying your collected water.

4. Fill the Container

Deliver the water to the team's container and contribute toward the shared objective.

Installation

Water Cup is a CounterStrikeSharp plugin for Counter-Strike 2. 

1. Install CounterStrikeSharp on your CS2 server.

2. Build Water Cup:

dotnet build -c Release

3. Copy the compiled plugin:

bin/Release/net10.0/WaterCup.dll

4. Place it in your CounterStrikeSharp plugins directory:

game/csgo/addons/counterstrikesharp/plugins/WaterCup/

5. Start or restart your CS2 server.
